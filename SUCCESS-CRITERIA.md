# Rishi's First Birthday: Success Criteria

Each item is scored 0–10. The bar to ship is an average of **9.0 or higher, with no item below 8**.

| # | Criterion | What "10" looks like |
|---|-----------|----------------------|
| 1 | **Visual style matches the references** | Cream card, thin textured green frame, confetti dots, sun top-left and leafy branch top-right, a big red collage "1" with the caterpillar arched over it, and a fruit row with eaten holes. Every colored surface uses painted tissue-paper texture. Nothing flat or vector-generic. |
| 2 | **Caterpillar looks like the book** | Overlapping painted green segments with teal accents, fine colored bristles, small brown feet, a red head with yellow-ringed green eyes, a small nose, and purple/blue antennae. It breathes, blinks and inches along. |
| 3 | **Butterfly looks like the book** | Wide wings with blue/teal upper wings, green and yellow rays, pink/orange eye spots, dotted navy edges, long purple lower wings with yellow-and-red eye spots, and a tan body with a red head. Wings flap with an organic, slightly lagging stroke. |
| 4 | **Typography matches the references** | Serif lowercase lines ("our very hungry little", "first birthday"), heavy geometric caps with a different collage texture on each letter, spaced small caps for the details, and a slab "1". Clear hierarchy that fits one phone screen. |
| 5 | **Invite works on its own** | Who, what, when and where read in under 5 seconds: Rishi, first birthday, Sunday Nov 22, 3:00 PM, 2459 NE Daphne St, Issaquah. Add to calendar and Directions work. Skipping the game costs one tap. |
| 6 | **First-run UX** | Asks for a name with a clear reason, offers "Play" or "Skip to the invite", pre-fills the name for returning guests, and shows a helpful inline error instead of blocking silently. |
| 7 | **Touch gameplay** | One-thumb control: touch or hold where you want him to go. Turning is smooth and capped, he bends softly away from walls, never dies unfairly, has generous eating hitboxes, holds 60fps, and the page doesn't scroll or zoom during play. |
| 8 | **Book narrative** | Monday through Sunday food waves (1 apple … 5 oranges, Saturday treats, a tummy ache, the Sunday leaf), with the day banner and a HUD showing each day's foods as they are eaten. He grows longer and fatter. |
| 9 | **Metamorphosis** | One moment: the body gathers in, the cocoon wraps and wobbles, a paper-confetti burst, wings unfold with spring, a graceful banked flight, and he lands on the invite and keeps flapping. |
| 10 | **Leaderboard and nav** | Results show the time and rank. The scores view shows best time per guest with your row highlighted and an honest empty state. Navigation is simple: Invite ↔ Play ↔ Scores. |
| 11 | **Motion quality** | Exponential/spring easing, no jank, no layout shift, staggered pop-ins, `prefers-reduced-motion` respected. |
| 12 | **Polish** | Themed selection, caret and focus rings; safe-area insets; works at 360–430px widths and on desktop (centered card on a confetti table). |
